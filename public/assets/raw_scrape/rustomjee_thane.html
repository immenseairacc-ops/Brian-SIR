Thane's Position Within the MMR

Thane is a metropolitan city in the northwestern part of Maharashtra. It is the headquarters of the Thane district and is a part of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR).

Located in the northeastern part of Mumbai, Thane offers seamless connectivity to all parts of Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. Moreover, it is a prime reason for the city’s transformation from an extension of Mumbai’s suburbs to a dynamic urban real estate hub.        

 

Transportation & Connectivity

Thane has a robust public transportation system consisting of buses across 45 routes. This city also has its own railway station, which connects to areas like Panvel, Nerul, Vashi, Mumbai, Pune, and Nashik. In terms of connectivity, roadways like the Thane Belapur Highway, Eastern Express Highway, Ghodbunder Road, JVLR, and SCLR facilitate hassle-free commuting.

As a result, there is high demand for both under-construction and ready-to-move flats in Thane West, making it an ideal destination for real estate investors.
