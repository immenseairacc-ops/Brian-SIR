Frequently Asked Questions

Rustomjee Crown is located in Prabhadevi, in central South Mumbai. The site address is Rustomjee Crown, Gokhale Road South, Prabhadevi, Mumbai - 400 025. The 5.75-acre gated estate sits between Worli to the south and Dadar to the north, with the Arabian Sea bordering Prabhadevi on the west. The Siddhi Vinayak Temple is approximately 1.3 KM away and High Street Phoenix 3.2 KM.

Prabhadevi is centrally located between Worli, Dadar and the Arabian Sea, offering quick access to South Mumbai's business districts, the Bandra-Worli Sea Link to BKC, and rail connectivity via Mahalaxmi Station and Dadar. The micro-market is established for luxury housing, with reputed hospitals like Hinduja, Breach Candy and Wockhardt nearby and retail anchors at High Street Phoenix, Palladium, Atria Mall and Kamla Mills.

Rustomjee Crown offers 3 BHK, 4 BHK and 5 BHK residences across three towers, with two towers rising to 68 floors and one to 67 floors. Ceiling heights soar to 11 feet 9 inches and 10 feet 6 inches, supporting expansive open layouts. The 4 and 5 BHK homes start at ₹17.01 Cr, all inclusive. Contact Rustomjee for current pricing on the 3 BHK homes and for a specific floor or view.

Rustomjee Crown is delivered in two MahaRERA-registered phases. Phase 1, covering Towers A and B, is registered under P51900003268 and has received its Occupancy Certificate. Phase 2, covering Tower C, is registered under P51900006367 and is under construction. Both registrations are listed on the MahaRERA portal under registered projects, where buyers can verify current status, certified plans and quarterly progress.

Rustomjee Crown is centrally located for South Mumbai access. Worli and Lower Parel are immediately south and well connected by road; Dadar is to the north; Mahalaxmi Station sits about 7.2 KM away. The Bandra-Worli Sea Link gives quick access to BKC and the western suburbs. Both Eastern and Western Express Highways are reachable, and Nariman Point and the commercial belts of Lower Parel are easily commutable.

Rustomjee Crown is designed by Hafeez Contractor, one of India's most prominent architectural practices. Interiors are crafted by PIA Interiors, a Bangkok-based firm working across Southeast Asia, featuring premium fittings, imported marble and smart-home integrations. Ceilings are 11 feet 9 inches and 10 feet 6 inches, framing expansive views and giving each residence a boundless feel. Every square foot is calibrated for easy luxury living.

Rustomjee Crown offers more than 60 lifestyle amenities. Highlights include a poolside amphitheatre, a squash court, an outdoor dining deck, a ballroom, a private cinema, a well-equipped gym, a kids' creative corner and a yoga deck. Outdoor zones include a Lagoon Pool, Aroma Garden, Hot Tubs, Solitude Forest, Sunken Court, Cloudwalk and English Court. Additional amenities feature a lounge cafe, multi-play court, floating lounge and indoor games room.

Bombay Scottish School is 3.1 km from Rustomjee Crown, and Prabhadevi's position between Worli and Dadar keeps the rest of South Mumbai's schools within reach by road. For healthcare, Hinduja Hospital is 3 km from the estate, Wockhardt Hospitals 5 km and Breach Candy Hospital 6.8 km. The Siddhi Vinayak Temple is 1.3 km away.

Kamla Mills is 2.2 km from Rustomjee Crown, and Palladium and High Street Phoenix are both 3.2 km away. Atria Mall is 4.1 km from the estate. For leisure, the St. Regis hotel is 3.1 km away, and the Mahalaxmi Race Course and the NSCI are each 5 km.

Residences at Rustomjee Crown

Crown is two propositions on one estate. Towers A and B are complete and hold their Occupancy Certificate, so a buyer there is purchasing a finished home. Tower C is the second phase of the project, under construction and registered separately with MahaRERA.

Across the three towers the homes run 3 BHK, 4 BHK and 5 BHK. The 3 BHK homes sit in Tower C, and the 4 and 5 BHK homes in Towers A and B. Ceilings rise to 11 feet 9 inches and 10 feet 6 inches, which gives the larger homes room for open living areas and walk-in closets. Unit plans and floor plans are published on the Rustomjee Crown residences page.

Design at Rustomjee Crown

Hafeez Contractor is the principal architect, and the floor-to-ceiling heights of 11 feet 9 inches and 10 feet 6 inches were planned with him to frame the views from each home. The interiors are by PIA Interiors, a design firm based in Bangkok that works across Southeast Asia.

Inside the homes, the specification brings together premium fittings, imported marble and smart home integrations. The design approach and interior specification are set out on the Rustomjee Crown design page.

Amenities at Rustomjee Crown

The amenities are spread across the 5.75-acre estate. Highlights include a poolside amphitheatre, a squash court, an open-air dining deck, a kids' creative corner, a ballroom, a well-equipped gym and a private cinema.

The wider list adds a lounge and cafe, an English court, a yoga deck, an indoor games room, a multi-play court, a floating lounge and a solitude forest. The poolside amphitheatre is the unusual one: a seating deck at the water's edge that turns the pool surround into a place for evening gatherings as well as swimming.

Each space is shown on the Rustomjee Crown amenities page.
