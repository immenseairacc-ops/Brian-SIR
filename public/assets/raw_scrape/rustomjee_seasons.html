Frequently Asked Questions

Rustomjee Seasons is located in BKC Annexe near Kala Nagar, Bandra East, Mumbai. The site address is Rustomjee Seasons, Nanasaheb Dharmadhikari Road, Near MIG Club, Kala nagar, BKC Annexe, Mumbai-400 051. The 3.8-acre gated community sits between the Bandra-Worli Sea Link and the Western Express Highway, with Gurunanak Hospital 350 metres away.

Rustomjee Seasons is sold out except for Sky Chambers, a duplex penthouse in Wing D priced at ₹35 Cr, all inclusive. The penthouse carries a 4,576 sq. ft. carpet area across a lower duplex level and an upper duplex level. Wing D is one of six wings in the 3.8-acre gated community, which has received its occupation certificate.

The MahaRERA registration number for Rustomjee Seasons Wing D is P51800021028. Wing A and Wing D RERA certificates are individually published on rustomjee.com as downloadable PDFs. Buyers can verify the project registration, promoter details, and quarterly progress disclosures on the official Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority portal. The project also publishes an Environment Clearance.

Rustomjee Seasons offers a swimming pool, a toddlers' creative studio, a forest walk, a jogging track, a fully-equipped gym, a multipurpose court, a tennis court, a mini-theatre, a maze garden, an indoor games room, a banquet hall, a health cafe, a senior citizen zone with reflexology path, an alfresco dining area, and a performance studio. The 3.8-acre site includes an open-to-sky podium and a kids' play area.

Rustomjee Seasons was designed by architect Sanjay Puri, a Mumbai-based architect whose Sanjay Puri Architects firm is widely recognised for residential, commercial, and institutional projects across India and internationally. The 3.8-acre layout is a gated community of six wings with an open-to-sky podium and a forest walk, and the estate has received its occupation certificate.

BKC Annexe is the residential belt adjoining Bandra Kurla Complex, India's planned commercial business district that houses the National Stock Exchange, the Bombay Stock Exchange (Bandra location), Jio World Centre, and offices of Google, Facebook, and major Indian banks. Rustomjee Seasons is one of the few 3.8-acre gated communities in this BKC Annexe belt, distinct from single-tower projects in the same locality.

Rustomjee Seasons is 1.3 km from the Western Express Highway and 3.9 km from the Bandra-Worli Sea Link. The Santacruz Chembur Link Road route to the Central Suburbs is 5.1 km away and the Eastern Express Highway 7.6 km. The International Airport is 5.6 km from the estate and the Domestic Airport 6.1 km.

Ascend International School is 1.4 km from Rustomjee Seasons, Mount Litera International School 2.3 km, the American School of Bombay 3 km and Dhirubhai Ambani International School 3.1 km. Gurunanak Hospital is 350 metres away and Asian Heart Institute 1.8 km. For shopping, Jio World Drive is 1.5 km away, Jio World Plaza 3.1 km and Phoenix Market City 5.8 km.

Rustomjee Seasons at a Glance

Rustomjee Seasons is a completed 3.8-acre gated community of six wings in BKC Annexe, Bandra East. The development has received its Occupancy Certificate and its apartments are sold out. One home remains available: Sky Chambers, a duplex penthouse in Wing D with 4,576 sq ft of carpet area, at ₹35 Cr, all inclusive.

Sky Chambers, the Remaining Duplex Penthouse

Sky Chambers is a duplex penthouse in Wing D, laid out across a lower and an upper level with 4,576 sq ft of carpet area in all. An internal staircase joins the two floors, so the home works as a private house within the tower rather than as a single-floor apartment.

Because the wider community is complete and occupied, a buyer moves into a settled estate rather than a construction site. The unit plan and the floor plans for the lower and upper duplex levels are published on the Rustomjee Seasons residences page.

Amenities at Rustomjee Seasons

Six wings across 3.8 acres leaves room for a full amenity programme rather than a single podium deck. The estate has a swimming pool, a well-equipped gym, a home theatre for movie nights, an indoor games room and a banquet hall for birthdays and anniversaries.

Families with young children have a toddlers' creative studio and a kids' play area. A jogging track and a forest walk run through the greenery between the buildings, and a senior citizen zone with a reflexology path, a performance studio and an alfresco dining area complete the set.

Each space is shown on the Rustomjee Seasons amenities page.
