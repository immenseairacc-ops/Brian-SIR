What is the starting price of the Apartment in “The Westpark”?

The starting price for the apartment in The Westpark is available upon request. Please contact our customer care for detailed information.

Why should I consider buying an Apartment in “The Westpark” by DLF?

“The Westpark”, a joint venture between DLF and Trident marks the entry of DLF in Mumbai. The aspiration and lifestyle DLF have offered over the years in the other cities of India, the same and even better offerings, we commit to deliver with our project “The Westpark”.

What are the configurations of apartments and amenities offered in “The Westpark”?

The Apartments in “The Westpark” are generously sized 3, 4 and 5 bedrooms, where full-height windows, soaring ceilings, and elegant glass-balustrade balconies create a seamless connection to the outdoors. These homes are private sanctuaries: airy, refined, and designed for contemporary living.

Spacious living and dining areas, along with serene master bedrooms, define each home. Select layouts also include a flexible study space, with interiors designed to suit every aspect of life, from work and rest to entertaining.

  • 3, 4 and 5 bedroom configurations
  • Double glazed windows ensuring sound insulation
  • Integrated charging points
  • Balconies with glass balustrades
